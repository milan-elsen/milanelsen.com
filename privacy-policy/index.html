Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to our website. We take your privacy very seriously. You can use this website without providing any personal information, and we do not store any personal data about you. Two services help us run the site: GitHub Pages hosts it, and GoatCounter counts page views. Both are described below.

Contact Information
If you have any questions regarding this privacy policy, please contact us via email at apps@milanelsen.com

Imprint: https://milanelsen.com/imprint/

Hosting provided by GitHub Pages
Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect data such as access logs (e.g., IP addresses, browser type, and access times) as part of their hosting services. This data is not accessible to us and is managed entirely by GitHub.
Place of processing: United States
GitHub Privacy Policy 

Analytics
To see which pages are actually read, we count page views using GoatCounter. GoatCounter does not use cookies and does not store anything on your device, no cookies, no local storage, no cache entries. It does not store your IP address, your full browser identifier, or any tracking ID, so neither we nor GoatCounter can identify you or follow you across websites.

For each page view, the following is recorded: the page address, the website that referred you, your browser and operating system, your screen width, your language, and your country. The country is derived from your IP address, which is used only for that purpose and is not stored. To avoid counting the same visit twice, a randomly generated session identifier is kept in GoatCounter’s memory for up to 8 hours; it is not stored on your device and is not linked to you. Only anonymous totals are saved.

Place of processing: European Union (servers in Germany and Finland (Hetzner Online GmbH). The service is operated by Martin Tournoij, Ireland.)

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in understanding which pages are read and how the site is found (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). Because no personal data is stored, this analysis does not require your consent.
You can prevent the counting at any time by blocking the script with any content blocker, or by writing to us.

Your Rights Under GDPR
Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to object to processing based on legitimate interest, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.
